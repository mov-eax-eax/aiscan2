HOW TO FISH

1 · CAST

Hold the action key to charge, release to throw. The ring on the water is a school — land within 3 m of it for double bites. The marker on the power bar shows the throw you need.

2 · SINK

Set the lure depth with ↑ ↓, the wheel or the slider. Each fish lives in a depth band, and a feeding layer drifts through the water — keep the lure inside it for faster bites. Pick a lure with 1–3: the minnow is balanced, the jig sinks fast and loves the layer, the glow plug trades bites for rarer fish. Sonar shows blips; the scan line below the lure box names what is in the band.

3 · STRIKE

When the bobber plunges, strike inside the window. Too slow and the fish takes the bait. A strike is a click, Space, or the REEL button. While waiting you can jig the lure with a click to stir a bite — but jigging during the cooldown spooks the school. Press R (or the RECALL button) to reel in and try another cast, depth or position.

4 · FIGHT

Hold to reel: the fish rises, tension climbs. Let go to rest. When it surges, ease off or hold SHIFT to give line. Keep tension in the pale band; red for too long and the line snaps. Fish have behaviours — runners surge often, divers drop, thrashers jerk, deadweights grind.

5 · LAND

Reel the fish all the way to the surface. Deep fish take longer and pay more. Fish stamina drains fastest in the ideal tension band — tiring it out stops the surges.

STARS & SAVE

One star for the quota, a second for finishing inside the cast limit, a third for losing nothing. Consecutive catches build a coin streak. Progress autosaves in this browser with a backup slot and a resumable run checkpoint; EXPORT SAVE in the harbour keeps a file for future releases.
