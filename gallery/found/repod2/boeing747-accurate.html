Drag orbit · Right-drag pan · Scroll zoom. 7° wing dihedral, constant-radius fuselage with upper-deck hump, swept empennage — all lofted from sampled Catmull-Rom profiles.
Engineering notes: Fuselage and hump are generated by lofting circular cross-sections whose radius & top-lift are read from two Catmull-Rom curves. Wings / fin / stabilizers are lofted symmetric airfoil sections (NACA thickness law) along the swept, dihedraled span. Engines are pods lofted along their axis, hung on pylons below the wing lower surface.
