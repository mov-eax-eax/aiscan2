Stage 2 — the voids

The tower is hollow from the ground to the 2nd platform, and a hollow box above it. Members that cross those spaces are the recurring fault.

1 · Vertical section through the axis

Dark = material. Hatched = void. The two horizontal bars are the only members that cross the space between the four uprights.

2 · Plans — where the material is, at nine heights

Below the 2nd platform: four isolated legs and a cross-shaped through-void. Above it: a hollow square ring.

3 · The rules, as generator assertions

R-1 — no member connects two different legs below 115.73 m, except the two belts.
Eiffel: « quatre montants isolés, simplement reliés au niveau du 1er et 2e étage par des ceintures horizontales », and « il est inutile d'entretoiser la Tour dans les divers plans horizontaux ». The builder records each member's two endpoint legs; anything touching two different legs below L2 that is not a belt throws.
R-2 — no member's body may fall inside the core void.
isVoidAt(x, z, y) is true for the space bounded by the legs' inner faces, ground → 115.73 m, and for max(|x|,|z|) < innerHalf above it. Members are point-sampled along their length; any sample inside a void rejects the member.
R-3 — the leg interior is hollow too.
Each leg is a caisson with 800 mm clear internally. Nothing may be placed inside a leg except the lift rails and the stair, which are documented as being there.
R-4 — the only decorative exception is the four arches.
They cross the void by design, and they are the only members allowed to.

These are assertions in src/build/, not conventions. The harness reports any violation with the member's identity and the rule it broke.

4 · How empty is each level?

Void fraction is the empty share of the plan square, sampled on a 64 × 64 grid, counting only the structural material — so it excludes the lattice openings within a face, which are a separate (measured) quantity. The point is the shape of the curve, not the exact numbers: the tower is at its most hollow low down and closes up as it rises.
