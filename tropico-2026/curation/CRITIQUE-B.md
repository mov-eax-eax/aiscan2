## What the fixes did

- **01**: material pass only (mango/asymmetry, glass refrangement, machete bevel, concrete grit).
  Material truth 3→4. The curator's diagnosis stands: silhouette is the cause, material is the
  symptom, and the profiles were not redrawn. **29, short of the line.**
- **02**: the translucent near figure is now opaque and anchored (contact shadow, darker legs,
  no headless hat); the shade is three bounced lights; the sun is a bloom clipped to the tarp
  rather than two discs lying on it. Composition and light both move. **31→33.**
- **03**: the ranked fix was light, and it was applied hard: shadow bands from ~62% to ~41%,
  lit faces to ~95%, one broken fin casting a hard white blade of sun across four shadow bands,
  grain split per mass, two gravity-following stains. **31→34, first piece over the line.**

## Collection verdict

Seven works, seven languages, one problem. The canon holds: mangoes, tinacos, dishes, monobloc
chairs, solar panels, palms, cables and sea walls recur across realism, poster, tenebrism,
thermal and ornament, and the collection now has pieces where the sun actually hurts (02's
bleach, 03's blade of light) — the thing the round-A critique said was missing. What still does
not fully cohere is temperature: 02 and 03 both reach for coral *and* saturated teal, and the
one-accent rule was repeatedly treated as advice. The set is strongest when it behaves like an
inventory — 01's objects, 03's chair, 06's seed-cable, 07's predella are one world — and weakest
when it behaves like a portfolio of styles.

If there were one more round, it would be spent on the human figure. Four of the seven works
have a person in them and in every one the person is the weakest form: the officer's hands, the
fisherfolk's garments, the walkers' bodies. The country is convincing; its people are still
signs. That is the honest state of the collection at closing.
