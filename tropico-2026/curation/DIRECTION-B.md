# DIRECTION-B — supplement to the Lead's round-B briefs
Curator · 2026-10-17 · for 04 (maestro), 05 (colorist), 06 (vectorista)

Round B is already moving, so this does not replace the briefs you have. It only says, for each piece,
what to protect, the one trap to avoid, and what would make it win. Three short notes and one shared rule.

## 04 · EL FUNCIONARIO — neo-baroque tenebrism

**Protect.** The task light: light falls on the hands and the paper, the face is a half-shape. That is the
piece's whole idea (Artemisia, not Caravaggio's costume) and it is what keeps a bureaucrat from becoming a
portrait. Protect the fold as three values, and the paper as the single high-value anchor that lets the eye
measure the dark.

**The trap.** Costume. One velvet, one skull, one candle and it is a genre exercise. The baroque must be in
the *procedure* — the form stamped twice, the queue folding back on itself, a room furnished with nothing —
never in props. It is 2026 or it is nothing.

**What wins.** Darks painted as paint: four or five overlapping near-blacks (brown-black, green-black,
blue-black), visible edge break-up, a soft terminator against one hard blade of light, no flat black
anywhere. At 400 the light lands on the work. One doubled thing with a difference: two identical forms, one
stamped. No cyan; rust red used once.

## 05 · LA RED — post-digital / thermal / surveillance

**Protect.** The moving zero: the hottest thing in frame justifies every other value. Protect the window as
a blank (glass blocks thermal) and the instrument's off-centre, institutionally-justified frame. Protect the
flatness — this is a picture made of temperature zones, not of objects.

**The trap.** Predator vision. The moment it reads as a video-game HUD or as a normal painting with a colour
ramp laid over it, the sensor is a costume. Second trap: glitch as texture. Choose one artefact and commit
(8 px macroblocks, or a 2 px chroma offset, not both).

**What wins.** It must see what the eye cannot — the wall still warm at 16:40, the interior invisible behind
the glass — and it must be flat like a sensor: hard-edged temperature fields, no soft object shading. Cyan is
the instrument; orange exists only as temperature. The timestamp is unreadable marks.

## 06 · JARDÍN DE DATOS — cyber-botanical art nouveau

**Protect.** The grid, and the garden growing *through* it: one cell broken, one stem crossing a rule.
Protect conventionalised leaves — a family of curves, not a traced leaf (Owen Jones's rule, and the line
between art nouveau and clip-art). Protect the glasshouse: the Wardian case is the right structure, a
tropical garden held in a box.

**The trap.** Wallpaper. The moment the grid becomes a pattern and the plants become decoration, the piece is
a screensaver. Second trap: the data becoming an infographic — no labels, no arrows, no legend.

**What wins.** Information carried *in the form*: leaves ranked by size, a vine following a cable, petals
counted as days. A viewer must be able to read one rule without a caption. One specimen big enough to
examine at 200 px, one deliberate break in the grid.

## Shared, for all three

**Break the row.** Round A's three pieces all put their subject in a band across the lower third with a large
empty field above. 04: a vertical light column and a cropped figure. 05: the instrument's asymmetric frame.
06: a vertical garden grid. No piece may leave its top third empty.

**One of you must paint the glare.** The collection's thesis is that the sun is the debt, and round A produced
no piece where the sun actually hurts. 05 is the obvious candidate — the blown hottest point, the wall at
16:40 — but the win is available to any of the three.

**Same discipline.** 1600 × 1200, no legible text, grain last, a 400 px look after every pass, and the test
round A failed: one passage worth standing in front of at 1.2 m.
