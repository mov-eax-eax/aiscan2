# ESTRATOS — third room
### Ten works. Ten systems. Three distances each.

Rooms one and two were about **style** and then about **arrangement**. This room is about
**depth**: every piece must be built on a generative rule, must contain a nested system, and
must repay three different distances of looking. Variety is assumed; density is the assignment.

## The three-distance test (mandatory for every piece)

1. **FAR (400 px, thumbnail).** The archetype and the composition must read instantly. If it
   needs the caption, it fails.
2. **MIDDLE (arm's length, 1600 px).** The subject must be legible and complete: this is the
   picture a person thinks they are looking at.
3. **NEAR (nose to the glass).** There must be a **hidden system** that only resolves up close:
   a second encoding, a repeated module that mutates, a layer of marks that is readable only at
   100%, a figure hidden in the field, a border that counts something. It does not have to be
   explained in the caption; it has to be *there*.

## The rule requirement

Every piece must be generated by a rule you can state in one sentence — a module and a
permutation, a serial sequence, an encoding, a growth rule, a register. The rule must produce
**variation**, not wallpaper: at least three different states of the module must be visible, and
one deliberate **break** of the rule must be placed where it matters. State the rule in
`notes.md` and in `meta.json`.

## Complexity floor

- At least **three nested scales** (e.g. border > field > incident > detail).
- At least **one embedded counter or code**: something in the image that counts, dates, indexes
  or measures, drawn as geometry (no text unless the idiom requires letterforms; if it does,
  they are drawn as paths).
- At least **two registers** (e.g. main scene and marginalia; figure and ground; plan and
  elevation; map and legend).
- Target 250-900 vector elements. Density is not noise: every cluster must have a rule.

## Thinking budget

- **Before drawing**: write, in notes.md, one paragraph — *what this piece knows that the caption
  will not say* — and one sentence stating the generative rule. Do not skip this step.
- **8 to 12 passes**, not 4. Render and LOOK after every pass. At pass 3 and at pass 7, write a
  checkpoint in notes.md: what the far/middle/near readings currently are, and which one is failing.
- Run the three tests every few passes: 400 px thumbnail, 1600 px, and a 100% crop of the
  densest area.
- Converge, then stop. A deep finished piece beats an infinite one.

## Deliverables per piece

    estratos/<id>-<slug>/<slug>.svg            self-contained, viewBox as specified
    estratos/<id>-<slug>/render-<slug>.png      1600 px (portrait pieces: 1600 wide)
    estratos/<id>-<slug>/notes.md                argument, rule, pass log, three-distance checkpoints, critique
    estratos/<id>-<slug>/meta.json               id, title, style, idiom, rule, readings {far, middle, near},
                                                 palette, techniques, statement, artist

## The ten works

| # | Work | Idiom | What it must be |
|---|------|-------|-----------------|
| E1 | Carta Celeste | 17th-c. star atlas plate | a sky chart of a city: constellations as infrastructure, magnitudes as populations |
| E2 | Beato de la Sequía | Romanesque illuminated manuscript | a Beatus page for a 2026 drought: text block, miniatures, marginalia, gold, a maze |
| E3 | La Pared | palaeolithic cave wall | hand stencils and animals — with one modern object painted in the same ochre |
| E4 | La Ola de Datos | ukiyo-e woodblock | the great wave as a wave of cable/fibre, with a hidden register of marks |
| E5 | Lámina Anatómica | 19th-c. anatomical plate | an exploded anatomy of a machine that is also an organism, with numbered callouts |
| E6 | Seis Ventanas | six-panel comic page | six panels = six windows of one building, one object recurring at six scales |
| E7 | Rosetón | stained-glass rose window | cathedral tracery that is also a circuit diagram, leading as wiring |
| E8 | Marigrama | tide chart / marigram | a year of tides drawn as a mountain range, with a hidden harbour profile |
| E9 | Mosaico | Soviet mosaic panel | a heroic mosaic whose tesserae encode a second image at a distance |
| E10 | Semilla | seed-packet illustration | a botanical packet whose specimen is infrastructure, with a legend that is a calendar |

No two pieces may share a skeleton. No idiom may repeat from rooms one or two.
