# E3 LA PARED — notes
Artist: lead · palaeolithic wall · 1600x1200

## THE ARGUMENT
A wall of hands is the oldest census we have. This piece is a count, not a scene: fourteen
clusters of five, each cluster one generation smaller than the last, and in every cluster exactly
one hand is inverted - the one that did not survive the count. The wall is a record of people who
are otherwise absent. The only modern object is a quadcopter, in the clearing, painted in the same
ochre as the hands, so that at first glance it belongs to the same century as the rest.

## THE RULE
14 clusters x 5 hands. Size decreases by one step per hand in a cluster. Rotations advance by 18
degrees per hand. One inverted hand per cluster. One clearing is kept empty for the observer.

## PASS LOG
1. First build: animals (bison, horse, ibex) + hands. The animals failed - blobs with stick legs,
   neither palaeolithic nor animal. Two rebuilds of the anatomy did not fix it; the medium is fine
   for silhouettes but not for quadruped anatomy at this level of detail.
2. DECISION: remove the animals as the subject and keep them only as a faded palimpsest layer
   (opacity 0.14), which is honest - the old paintings on a cave wall are barely there. The subject
   becomes the census of hands.
3. Hands rebuilt as procedural stamps: five capsules for fingers, palm ellipse, spray optional.
   Cluster logic added (14 x 5, shrinking, inverted hand).
4. Hierarchy pass: three large anchor hands, tallies moved into empty ground, observer scaled up.

## THREE DISTANCES
FAR (400 px): a wall of hands, warm ochre and charcoal, denser at the edges - a crowd.
MIDDLE (1600 px): clusters of five, generations shrinking, some sprayed and some stamped.
NEAR (100%): the drone in the clearing; three tally clusters; one inverted hand in every cluster;
the faded animal outlines from an older layer.

## HONEST CRITIQUE
The three-distance structure is real, but the hands read as gloves rather than stencils: the finger
capsules are too regular, and a real stencil has the wrist cut off and the edges sprayed soft. The
composition is a field rather than an arrangement - the eye has no single place to land except the
observer's clearing, which is the point, but it is also an excuse. If it had another three passes:
irregular fingers per hand (no two hands identical), more negative stencils (spray around a hand),
and a stronger clearing.
