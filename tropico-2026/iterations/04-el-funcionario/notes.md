# NOTES — 04 EL FUNCIONARIO
Artist: maestro · neo-baroque tenebrism · viewBox 0 0 1600 1200

> Written up by the Lead from the artist's reports. The artist's session lost its tool sandbox
> after the final render and could not write this file; the picture and the render on disk are
> his, the prose below is his account, assembled from three reports.

## CONCEPT
A customs officer in a container yard at dusk, after the power cut. One hooded floodlight on a
pole; everything outside its cone falls to brown-black. He stands behind a makeshift desk of
paperwork: stacked forms, a rubber stamp, an ink pad, a laminated ID on a lanyard, a plastic
gallon of water, a dead monitor. Behind him, a dark wall of containers with taped forms.
Neo-baroque here means an excess of PROCEDURE, not ornament: the paper is the subject.

## THE DECISION
Put the paper above the man. The floodlight exists to examine forms, so the forms are the
brightest thing in the picture and the officer is a dark mass with one hard rim. His face is
sunk into shadow and his hands are silhouettes against the lit sheet, because the painting is
about procedure rather than about him.

## LIGHT
One hooded source upper left; a hard-edged diagonal shaft with dust; cold blue-green in the
shadow; paper as the brightest event. Chiaroscuro with a warm key and a cold shadow.

## PASS LOG (six passes, as reported)
1. Block-in: values only, figure as a mass.
2. Figure, desk and light shaft placed.
3. Anatomy correction (shoulders/arms), the ID lanyard.
4. The procedure: eleven taped forms, the desk sheets, the stamp, the dead monitor.
5. Hands failed as forms; inverted them into silhouettes against the bright sheet.
6. Settle: grain, edge control, the last tone.

## CRITIQUE
The light is the strength: one hooded source, a hard-edged diagonal shaft with dust, cold
blue-green shadow, paper as the brightest event. The figure is stiff and illustrative - an
anatomy problem, not a lighting one - and the hands, meant to be its second subject, ended as
dark lumps, which is why they were inverted into silhouettes. The "excess of procedure" reads as
a field of pale rectangles rather than an overwhelming fact.

SELF-SCORE 28/60: composition/value 5 · light 6 · material 3 · legibility 5 · craft 4 · risk 5.
Weakest single thing: the hands.
