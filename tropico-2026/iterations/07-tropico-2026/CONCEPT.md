# 07 — TRÓPICO 2026 (Retablo del Mediodía)
Lead's synthesis piece. Not another style experiment: the collection's argument, in one object.

## Form
A Spanish retablo / altarpiece structure, modernised:
  viewBox 2400 x 1500
  - left wing     (x 0..360)      an interior: shuttered light, fan, plastic chair, glass of water
  - centre panel  (x 420..1980)   the malecón at 16:40 — the public world, realism + light
  - right wing    (x 2040..2400)  the same street seen thermally / by the state — data
  - predella      (y 1260..1500)  seven small squares: the inventory, one object each
  - frame         thin ochre-red rules between panels, 8-10 units, never decorative

## Argument
Three ways of knowing one street: from inside (private, dark), from the street (public, blazing),
from above (the state, cold). The predella is the index of things that appear in all three.
The viewer is asked to notice that the same mango, machete and tinaco are, in the right wing,
just heat signatures.

## Palette
centre: limestone white / sea-salt grey / dusty palm / coral accent / oil-drum blue horizon
left wing: brown-black, one blade of #ffe7b0, a single #d9a05b bounce
right wing: near-black ground, cyan #22d3ee, magenta #ff2d78, white scan lines, 2% noise
predella: each square quotes the palette of the piece it comes from
frame: #7a2b18 dead ochre-red, matte

## Craft decisions
- One sun for the whole centre panel; the two wings are the *same instant* — left wing shadow
  direction must agree with the shuttered light, right wing must show the same street.
- The centre panel must read at 400 px as a place (sea wall, palms, pavilion, sea).
- No legible text except, possibly, one stencilled ministry mark, reduced to a graphic.
- The predella squares are painted, not iconographic: they must have the same grain and blur
  discipline as the big panels, just at 160x160.

## Failure risks
- reads as a design board rather than a painting -> keep one dominant light across everything
- thermal wing looks like a video-game HUD -> restrain it: 3 hues only, no fake UI text
- predella becomes a pattern -> vary the crop and the light event in each square
