# NOTES — 07 TRÓPICO 2026 (RETABLO)
Artist: lead · three-ink screenprint · viewBox 0 0 2400 1500

## WHY A PRINT AND NOT A PAINTING
The collection spent three pieces learning to paint and one to draw with geometry. The
synthesis could not be a fourth painting: it had to be the *object* the other six were
made for. A retablo is a printed devotional object, cheap, nailed to a wall, reproduced -
which is what a national image is. So the hero is a three-plate screenprint on paper:
ink, vermilion, teal, ochre, on a cream stock.

## THE ARGUMENT
Left wing: the street outside, high key, whitewashed wall, a chair in the sun.
Centre: the interior at siesta, one doorway onto blinding sea light, a figure standing
in it, the inventory on a table, a dog asleep in the light.
Right wing: the same doorway seen thermally, by a camera, by the state.
Predella: seven printed objects - the same inventory, as a register of saints.
The three panels are one instant. The viewer is asked to notice that the mango, the
machete and the tinaco are, in the right wing, only heat.

## PASS LOG
1. Tried a painterly triptych (gradients, blur, grain). Failed: SVG gradients produced
   plastic surfaces, the centre panel read as cartoon, and the whole thing competed
   badly with the flat-colour pieces in the collection.
2. Rebuilt the centre as a threshold (dark room, door, figure) - composition worked,
   surface still failed.
3. DECISION: convert the whole object to a three-ink screenprint. Replaced every
   gradient with flat plates, added halftone and hatch patterns for shading, paper
   stock, misregistered red keyline on the door, print grain over everything.
4. Corrected frame rules and predella dividers, dropped the light cone that read as
   fog, moved the figure onto the threshold, removed three failed additions (a shirt
   pictogram, light bars on the wall, a chair that read as a wedge).

## WHAT I WOULD STILL FIX
The outside view through the door is still too plain - it needs one more event (a
kerb, a boat, a person) to stop reading as a colour field. The predella objects are
slightly iconographic; the tinaco and the dish want one more hour each.

## REGISTRATION NOTE
The red keyline sits 6-10 units out of register from the ink plate, deliberately:
that offset is what makes it read as a print rather than a drawing.
