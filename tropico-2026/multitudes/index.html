The rule of the room. Density is not clutter: every figure has a role, every cluster answers to a stated crowd rule, and each scene must hold three simultaneous actions with no indication of which one we are supposed to watch. At 400 px it is a place. At 1600 px it is several places at once. At 100% it is individual people with things in their hands — and somewhere in there, a motif repeated a fixed number of times. The card prints the rule and the count, so the claim is testable; the caption never says where they are.
