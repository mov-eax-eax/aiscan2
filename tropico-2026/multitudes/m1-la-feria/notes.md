# M1 — La Feria

**Idiom:** Bruegelian village panorama, 1600x1200.

## Argument

A town square on festival day, seen from slightly above the crowd. The fair is the excuse; the
picture is the machinery of a public day — market, procession, a fight, children, a cart on its
side, a gallows outside the wall, a mill on the hill. The eye is not told where to stop. The
crowd is thickest where the day is most official (the church door) and thinnest where the day is
most official in another way (the gallows). Nine red caps move through the scene doing nine
different jobs: market woman, porter, child, carter, merchant, miller, watcher at the gallows,
banner carrier, foreground carrier.

## Crowd rule

The crowd thickens toward the church door and thins toward the gallows: density is a sum of two
Gaussian attractions (church door, market row) minus a Gaussian repulsion centred on the gallows,
sampled over jittered points and thinned by a clearing mask around the stalls and the incidents.
Figures cluster in groups of 2–4 near the market.

## Find-it

One red cap, nine times, nine jobs (listed above). Not announced in the picture.

## Three simultaneous actions

1. A religious procession with a baldachin and banners crosses behind the market (left to centre).
2. Two men fight at the right edge while four onlookers stand around them.
3. Children chase a dog at the left, and a cart lies overturned in the foreground with its load
   spilled — a fourth, quieter action: a woman with a basket walks out of the frame.

## Pass log

1. **Build 1.** Architecture, ground, a first crowd of stick figures, stalls, incidents. Looked:
   value structure fine, figures all identical, incidents lost.
2. **Build 2.** New figure function (skirts, children, hats, arms, props, bundles), jittered
   clustered crowd with the density rule. Looked: the crowd read as a crowd; everything else was
   buried.
3. **Build 3.** Clearings for the stall row and the incidents; hats no longer red by default;
   stalls enlarged. Looked: the market row became visible; the crowd no longer swallowed it.
4. **Build 4 — CHECKPOINT.** FAR: a village with a church, a windmill and a mill; the market
   reads. MIDDLE: the stalls, the crowd, the procession, the fight, the cart are all findable.
   NEAR: figures have skirts, hats, baskets, bundles. **Failing: architecture is flat; the stalls
   read as tables on stilts; the value masses are all mid-tone.**
5. **Build 5.** Proper stall function (canopy, counter, posts, goods); lowered stall row; bigger
   gallows and windmill; the nine red caps placed; foreground figures and a bigger fight.
6. **Build 6.** Value pass: shadows under every figure, haze over the background, darker near
   ground, warm light pool on the square, sky bands.
7. **Build 7.** Density contrast sharpened (a distinct church-door crowd of 34); gallows side
   emptied; atmospheric wash.
8. **Build 8 — CHECKPOINT.** FAR: a crowded fair in a walled town, church dominant, mills on the
   hill; the eye goes to the striped market row. MIDDLE: procession with baldachin, the fight, the
   dog chase, the cart, the church door crowd. NEAR: red caps, baskets, bundles, faces as shape and
   light. **Failing: the figures are still types rather than characters; the architecture is
   schematic.**

## Three-distance result (final)

- **FAR:** a walled town square, church, windmill, market row, dense crowd — a place and a mood.
- **MIDDLE:** three stories (procession, fight, dog chase) plus the cart; the crowd rule is
  visible (dense at the church, empty at the gallows); the nine red caps can be hunted.
- **NEAR:** figures with poses, hats, skirts, baskets, bundles; the stalls' counters and goods; the
  baldachin; the gallows; the washing lines with clothes.

## Critique

- The figures are pictograms. At 400 px that is a working crowd; at 100% they have no faces and
  only a few poses. A Bruegel crowd lives on posture and profile; this crowd lives on colour.
- The architecture is schematic: the church is a box with a rose window, the houses are boxes with
  cross-bracing. The plaza has no real perspective construction — the cobble lines are decoration,
  not a grid.
- The crowd rule reads, but as a statistical field rather than as choreography; nobody is grouped
  around an event except at the nine hand-placed incidents.
- The value structure is better but still even: sky, ground and crowd sit within two tones of each
  other, and the fight and the cart do not punch out.
- The find-it works: the red caps are small enough to be missed at 400 px and countable at 1600.
