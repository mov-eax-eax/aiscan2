# M2 — El Puente

**Idiom:** ukiyo-e crowd print, 1600x1200. 45 figures on the bridge, 3 boats, 7 herons.

## Argument

The bridge is a machine for sorting people. The print is less about the crossing than about the
bays: eight bays of five figures, each bay a unit of the bridge's rhythm, and the umbrellas moving
through them in a fixed colour sequence. Rain flattens the world into two inks (indigo and paper)
and makes the far bank a rumour — lanterns, a festival, a crowd too small to read. Below, the
river does the work the bridge avoids: a fisherman hauls a net; boats cross where there is no bridge.

## Crowd rule

The bridge recedes in eight bays of five figures; scale falls with each bay. Every figure carrying
an umbrella takes the next colour in a fixed sequence — red, indigo, cream — so the umbrellas are
a register running up the bridge.

## Find-it

A small white heron appears seven times, always at the edge of the frame: left edge, top edge,
right edge, bottom-right corner, bottom-left, upper-left and lower-right. Different scales, two
poses (standing and flying).

## Three simultaneous actions

1. The crossing: porters, a samurai, a monk, a child and a dog move both ways along the bays.
2. Below the bridge, a fisherman in a boat hauls a net.
3. On the far bank, a festival: lanterns are strung and a crowd has gathered by the pavilion.

## Pass log

1. **Build 1.** Bridge geometry, eight bays of five, the umbrella sequence, water, far bank,
   rain, herons, boats. Looked: the bridge and the crowd read at 400 px; the far bank was a hard
   green wedge; the fisherman was hidden under the deck.
2. **Build 2 — CHECKPOINT.** FAR: a bridge in rain with a colourful crowd. MIDDLE: the bays and
   the umbrella register read; the far bank reads as a place. NEAR: figures have robes, hats and
   bundles; the herons are countable. **Failing: the fisherman and a foreground anchor; the shore
   edge is a cut.**
3. **Build 3.** Fisherman and boat moved into open water; a large cropped porter anchors the
   foreground; railings darkened; lantern reflections added.
4. **Build 4 — CHECKPOINT.** FAR: bridge, rain, water, festival. MIDDLE: three actions readable
   (crossing, fishing, festival). NEAR: umbrella sequence, heron poses, the fisherman's net.
   **Failing: the bridge deck is a flat plank; the water is undifferentiated; the figures are
   types.** Converged here (pass budget cut by the session).

## Three-distance result

- **FAR:** a diagonal bridge in rain, a dense band of umbrellas, indigo water below, a lit bank.
- **MIDDLE:** eight bays of five, the umbrella register, boats and fisherman, the festival bank.
- **NEAR:** robes, hats, bundles, porter poles; the net; the herons' legs and beaks; the lantern
  strings.

## Critique

- The bridge has no structure: it is a flat plank with two rails. There are no piles, no underside,
  no shadow under the deck — the picture's architecture is weaker than its crowd.
- The water is a single blue field with wave lines; it needs currents, reflections, rain rings.
- The figures are types: robe, head, hat, umbrella. At 100% there is no face, no hand, no posture
  beyond the umbrella arm.
- The herons are the best part; they are drawn differently from the crowd (naturalistic) and they
  reward the search along the frame edges.
