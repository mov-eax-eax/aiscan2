# EL HOTEL — argument, rules, pass log

**Artist:** aereo · **Idiom:** cutaway / dollhouse · **Canvas:** 1600×1200

## Argument (written before drawing)

What this piece knows that the caption will not say: *a hotel is a machine for making strangers
private in public*. The cutaway is the building's own fantasy — every room at once, no door closed —
and the organising fact of the picture is that the light is doing the telling: each room is lit from
inside, and the temperature alternates warm/cool floor by floor, so the building reads at a glance as
six stacked bands of living and, up close, as thirty separate dramas that never speak to each other.
The hotel is one organism and twenty unrelated lives; the only thing that crosses a floor is the
corridor, and the only thing that crosses the whole building is a cat.

## Crowd rule

**Rooms are lit from within; the light temperature alternates warm / cool by floor.** Every room's
interior is a flat lit field (no exterior daylight on the back walls), so at 400 px the hotel is six
horizontal bands of alternating temperature, and any room's mood is set before its story is read.
The ground floor breaks the rule deliberately: lobby warm, bar cool, one room — the wedding — lit
warmer than its floor.

## Find-it motif

**A cat in eleven rooms, always at a different height in the frame.** Black silhouette, pale eyes:
on a bed, a table, a bath rim, a suitcase, a windowsill, a wardrobe, a floor, a lap, a crib, a bar
stool, a corridor. No announcement.

## Three simultaneous actions

1. **The wedding**, third floor, left: a bride and groom and four guests in a room too small for them.
2. **The burglary**, fifth floor, right: a figure coming in over the balcony rail while a guest sleeps.
3. **The leak**, second floor, middle: a bath overflowing into the corridor while a cleaner runs for it.
   (Ground floor: the taxi waiting under the canopy, its meter running — the only clock in the picture.)

## Pass log**Pass 1.** Building shell, thirty rooms on a warm/cool alternation, street, taxi, roof props, eleven cats. Saw: the value structure worked at 400 px immediately (six lit bands in a dark frame); the people were blocks; the roof props floated in the sky; the ground floor was one empty room.

**Pass 2.** Rebuilt the figure function (head with hair, shoulders, arms, stance) and scaled the cast up; moved the roof props down onto the deck; fixed the canopy posts to the ground; filled the lobby and bar. Saw: it now reads as a hotel full of people, and the taxi gives the street a focal warm. Still missing: the three actions were not legible as actions.

**Pass 3.** The wedding got an arch and five guests and a warmer wall than its floor; the burglary got an open window, a rope and a figure at the sill; the bath got a mop and a stream; the entrance got a lit doorway, a doorman, a bellhop and a puddle.

**Pass 4.** The leak was a wire — one thin line down the facade. Rebuilt it as water: broken segments, a wide pale body, five puddles at the floor lines and a splash at the bath, a big puddle and a wet trail at the kerb. Lobby furniture, roof bulkhead and pipes so the roof is a place rather than a void.

## Checkpoint at pass 4

- **FAR (400 px): READS.** A six-storey cutaway in a dark frame, five alternating bands of warm and cool light over a lit street. The building is the structure and the lights are the subject. **Where the eye goes:** straight to the warm ground floor and the taxi — the lobby is the brightest thing and it is not where the story is.
- **MIDDLE (1600 px): READS, most of it.** The wedding, the burglary at the open window, the leak crossing the floors and the party are all findable without hunting; the bar and lobby are legible. **Failing:** the roof is still a dark band, and ten of the thirty rooms are quiet in the same way, so the middle of the plate has long calm stretches.
- **NEAR (100%): PARTLY PAYS.** Faces are suggested by light on a head, objects are in hands, the cats are on beds, sills and laps at different heights, and the leak has puddles with edges. **Failing:** the rooms' back walls are flat fields with nothing on them; a nose-to-the-glass viewer finds furniture and no dust.

**Pass 5.** Light last: exterior darkened, warm spill from the lobby and cool spill from the bar onto the wet pavement, the taxi headlights, two umbrellas, and the canopy shadow. Converged.

## Three-distance results (final)

- **FAR (400 px):** a hotel cutaway: six bands of alternating warm and cool light inside a dark frame, a red canopy, a yellow taxi, one pale line of water down the facade. The structure is instant; the mood is a wet night.
- **MIDDLE (1600 px):** thirty rooms; the wedding with its arch and five guests, the burglary at the open window with a rope, the overflowing bath with a mop and a stream that runs five floors to a puddle at the kerb, the wake with candles, the party, the bar, the lobby, the roof. Three actions are followable and none is signposted.
- **NEAR (100%):** figures with shoulders, hair and objects in hand; a cat on a sill, a bed, a lap, a bar stool, at eleven different heights; puddle edges; wet-street reflections.

## Honest self-critique

- **The roof is the weakest plane.** The tank stands above the parapet on legs that read as a table; the laundry is a row of coloured rectangles on a string.
- **Ten quiet rooms are too quiet.** Twenty rooms have an incident, but the empties read as unfurnished rather than as lives in another register.
- **The cutaway flattens the depth.** There is a street, a balcony rail, the rooms and a side wall, but no corridor and no perspective on the floors, so the building reads as a grid of lit boxes rather than a space to walk into.
- **The cats are findable but not charming.** Eleven heights is a good find-it thread; at 100 percent they are dark blobs with pale eyes.
- **What works:** the crowd rule. The warm/cool alternation does the whole picture at 400 px. The leak is the one line binding the floors, and the lit entrance under the red canopy is the front door in both senses.
