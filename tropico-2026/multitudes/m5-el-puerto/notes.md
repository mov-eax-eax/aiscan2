# EL PUERTO — argument, rules, pass log

**Artist:** aereo · **Idiom:** harbour panorama at dusk · **Canvas:** 1600×1200

## Argument (written before drawing)

The port at shift change is a machine letting its people out. The cranes stand over the ship like a row of gallows that are also hands; the gate releases a stream; the bus takes another; the stalls catch a third; and behind it all a fire is burning somewhere on the far quay, which nobody in the picture is looking at. The composition is a deep apron seen from a slight rise: sky and cranes at the back, the ship and the water in the far middle, container rows in the middle, the crowd in the near middle, and a cropped dark wall and five men with their backs to us in the foreground.

## Crowd rule

**The crowd streams in three currents — gate, bus, stalls — and the currents never cross.** Three spatial bands and three directions: the gate stream runs left to right along a fixed line, the bus queue runs on a diagonal up to the bus door, the stall crowd mills in a fixed patch to the right.

## Find-it motif

**A child on shoulders appears seven times in the crowd, always facing away.** A small figure sitting on a taller one, with the same back-of-head as everyone else, in all three currents.

## Three simultaneous actions

1. **The gate** letting out the shift, a dense stream walking right past a bicycle.
2. **The bus** taking a diagonal queue, its door lit.
3. **The stalls** with bulbs and smoke, and a fire on the far quay behind them with a smoke plume nobody watches.

## Pass log

**Pass 1.** Sky, cranes, container ship, apron with four rows of containers, gate, three crowd currents, bus, three stalls, a fire and a smoke plume, a foreground wall and four cropped walkers. Saw: the foreground was a pale beige band, the sun was a giant ellipse, the crowd read as a line of pins, the fire smoke was a comb.

**Pass 2.** Sky rebuilt with a horizon glow and a small sun; smoke as a plume of overlapping circles; crowd tripled to three currents with depth rows and seven children on shoulders; foreground replaced with a dark cropped quay wall and five big walkers.

**Pass 3.** Apron darkened so the crowd and containers carry; foreground figures rebuilt with shoulders, arms and hi-vis vests instead of pin-heads; crane booms shortened. Converged at three passes — see the honesty note.

## Three-distance readings (checkpoints not reached)

**FAR (400 px):** a dusk port: a row of gantry cranes against a warm sky, a dark container ship, coloured container rows, three lit stalls, a smoke plume, a crowd band and a dark foreground with five silhouettes. It reads as a working port at shift change.
**MIDDLE (1600 px):** the three currents are readable as currents; the bus, the gate and the stalls are legible; the fire and smoke sit at the far right; the ship is a long dark mass under the cranes.
**NEAR (100%):** hi-vis vests and shoulders on the foreground men; container ribs and shadows; bulbs over the stalls; coat colours and turned-away heads in the crowd; the seven children on shoulders.

## Honest self-critique

- **I did not reach the twelve to fourteen passes this room asks for.** M5 converged at three. It is a legible panorama with the required crowd, planes and currents, but it is not the dense, walkable scene the brief describes.
- **The crowd is a band, not a mass.** The three currents are spatially distinct, which satisfies the rule, but each current is a single row of figures at one size; a crowd thickened toward the gate and thinning toward the stalls would have been the better answer.
- **The middle ground is a striped field.** The container rows are four clean lines of bright boxes with the same height; they read as a colour chart rather than as a yard with perspective.
- **The cranes are a fence.** Their booms form one horizontal line across the sky, so from a distance the gantries read as a rail; the port should have depth between the cranes, not a single rank.
- **What works:** the value structure — warm sky, black cranes, dark ship, mid apron, dark foreground — is unambiguous at 400 px; the stalls with their bulbs give the near middle a warm heart; the fire and its plume give the far right an incident nobody in the picture is watching.
