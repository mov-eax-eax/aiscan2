# M9 — El Mural

**Idiom:** social-realist street mural (Rivera / Siqueiros), 1600x1200. 60+ figures, 3 registers.

## Argument

History compressed into one wall. The wall is read in three registers, each with its own scale:
below, labour (workers, a tractor, raised hands); in the middle, history (a march, a mother and
child, a crowd facing us, a skeleton in a sombrero); above, sky and machines (a red sun, gears, a
crane, a factory). The crowd faces the viewer at the centre and turns its back at the edges: the
wall looks at you where it can, and away where it must. The birds are the only thing on the wall
that is not painted.

## Crowd rule

Three registers, each with its own scale (bottom figures ~240-250 px, middle ~215-270 px, top
machines only); within the middle register the crowd faces the viewer in the centre and turns away
at both edges, so the wall has a centre of address and two margins of refusal.

## Find-it

One small naturalistic bird (not painted in the mural's style — a real pigeon/sparrow with
feathers, beak and eye) appears eight times: two in the top register (sky, crane), two in the
middle (the skeleton's sombrero, the crowd), four in the bottom (tractor, two workers, the hands).
Never announced.

## Three simultaneous actions

1. The march at the left, moving out of the frame with a red flag.
2. The central address: the crowd and the raised fist, the mother and child.
3. Labour below: the tractor and six workers with hammers and drills, plus the raised hands.

## Pass log

1. **Build 1.** Wall, three registers, machines, crowd, skeleton, tractor, birds. Looked: the
   registers read; the figures had detached hands and identical smiley faces; the skeleton was a
   tube.
2. **Build 2 — CHECKPOINT.** FAR: a three-band mural with sun, gears, crowd, skeleton, tractor.
   MIDDLE: the registers and the centre/edge facing rule read; the birds are findable. NEAR: the
   figures are crude; the arms were broken. **Failing: figure anatomy and the middle crowd's
   depth.**
3. **Build 3.** Arms rebuilt, hands attached, tools anchored, skeleton given ribs and arms, the
   central crowd widened, a back row added for depth, some profile faces, a raised fist and a red
   flag. Converged here (pass budget cut by the session).

## Three-distance result

- **FAR:** a three-register mural: red sun and machines above, a crowd and a skeleton in the middle,
  workers and a tractor below; the birds are visible as small grey marks.
- **MIDDLE:** the three registers and their scale difference; the crowd facing us at the centre and
  turning away at the edges; the march, the labour, the machinery.
- **NEAR:** the mural's plaster, cracks and patches; the faces (front, profile, back); the tools;
  the eight birds in naturalistic detail.

## Critique

- The figures are pictograms with smiley faces: the mural's monumentality is not there. Rivera's
  workers have weight and hands; these have circles and sticks.
- The three registers are hard bands, not a composition; the wall reads as a diagram of a mural.
- The birds are the strongest idea and the only element with a different drawing language; they
  are small enough to be missed, which is the point, but the rest of the wall does not reward the
  same attention.
- The skeleton is the closest thing to a real figure; it should have been the key to the whole
  register.
