WALL TEXT / 02

The rule. No two pieces in this room may share a skeleton. One is a diagonal race from corner to corner; one is made of letters; one is a map seen from straight above; one is a grid of twenty-four small frames; one is a circle inside a card; one is two images superimposed; one fills the frame with a single wing; one is almost entirely sky. If you can imagine two of them swapped onto the same layout, the room has failed.

The test. At thumbnail size you should be able to say which archetype you are looking at without reading the caption. That is the only shared standard here.
