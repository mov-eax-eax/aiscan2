# V8 OBSERVATORIO — notes
Artist: lead · silhouette against void · 1600x1200

## ARCHETYPE
Silhouette against void: 85% of the canvas is air, the horizon sits at four-fifths height, and
the subjects are three small dark shapes on the line. The composition is a big empty field with
a low cluster off to the right third.

## PASS LOG
1. Built the dusk gradient (seven stops), moon with halo, two mountain layers, ground band.
2. Placed the dome, the radio dish and the figure at the right third, with a long cast shadow
   coming toward the viewer; added a warm slit of light in the dome and a lamp in the figure's
   hand to give two tiny warm points against the cold sky.
3. Added the satellite trail as the only diagonal in the frame — it keeps the vast sky from
   being inert without giving it a subject.

## HONEST CRITIQUE
The archetype reads at 400 px, which is the test. The weakness is that the mountains are generic
triangles and the dish reads as a lollipop at a glance; the figure is a speck by design but the
piece leans on the two small warm lights more than it should. If it had another pass: a gravel
foreground texture and a less symmetrical mountain line.
